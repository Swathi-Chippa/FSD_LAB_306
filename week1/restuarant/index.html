Welcome to Sneha Koottu

സ്നേഹക്കൂട്ട് · Serving Fort Kochi Since 1986

In Malayalam, Sneha means love and Koottu means a blend. Located on the historic waterfront of Fort Kochi, we craft traditional Kerala recipes using hand-ground roasted spices, fresh coconut milk, and coastal recipes passed down through generations.
